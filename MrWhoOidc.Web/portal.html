Dev Portal

Customer Portal

This local portal signs in against MrWhoOidc and calls the protected MrWhoLicensing API. Use it to test onboarding plus the request, payment, and fulfillment flow in the development stack.

Local authority: https://localhost:8443/t/default. Licensing API: https://localhost:7443.

Sign in to the development portal

This uses Authorization Code + PKCE against your local MrWhoOidc dev instance. After login, the portal loads your licensing context from MrWhoLicensing.
