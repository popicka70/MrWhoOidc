Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Data We Collect

MrWhoOidc is a self-hosted identity provider. When you deploy MrWhoOidc, all identity data (users, credentials, tokens, sessions) stays on your own infrastructure. No data is transmitted to MrWhoOidc maintainers or any third-party service.

This Website

This informational website does not use cookies for tracking, does not collect personal data, and does not include third-party analytics.

Contact

If you have privacy-related questions, contact us at info@mrwhooidc.com.
