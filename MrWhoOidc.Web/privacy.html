Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Self-hosted installations

When you deploy MrWhoOidc, you operate the identity database and configure the service's integrations. External identity providers, email delivery, and telemetry exports may send data to services you configure. Your organization's privacy policy should describe how your installation processes identity data.

This website

The public information pages do not include third-party analytics or marketing tags. Requests to the website and its asset providers include connection metadata such as your IP address.

The site loads Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons from jsDelivr, and fonts from Google Fonts. Standard request metadata required to deliver those static assets may be processed by those providers.

Customer portal

The portal uses an account to manage organization details, billing email addresses, subscriptions, and license requests. Sign-in uses the MrWhoOidc service at mrwho.onrender.com. The portal stores sign-in session data in your browser; signing out clears that local session.

Payment QR codes are generated in your browser using a library served with this website. Their payment details are not sent to an external QR generation service.

Browser storage

mrwho.portal.session (local storage)
Contains sign-in tokens, their expiry, and account claims used by the portal. Local storage can survive closing the browser. The portal clears this entry when you sign out or when it handles an expired session. You can also remove it by clearing this site's browser data.
mrwho.portal.pkce (session storage)
Contains temporary values used to validate a sign-in response. The portal removes them after a successful sign-in or when clearing the session. Session storage is normally scoped to the browser tab.

Clearing browser storage does not delete your server-side account, subscription, or billing records. The sign-in service may also use cookies for its own login session.

Privacy requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, restrict or object to processing, or receive a portable copy. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Send questions about your data, retention, or a privacy request to info@mrwhooidc.com. Include the relevant account email and what you are requesting, but do not send passwords, tokens, or license keys.
