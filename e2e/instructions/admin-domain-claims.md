# Page: Domain Claims

## Route

`/admin/domain-claims`

## Expectations

- The page title clearly identifies tenant domain claims.
- The create form has a domain input, enrollment mode selector, and submit button.
- Existing claims are listed with domain, status, enrollment mode, verification timestamp, creator, and available actions.
- Validation or duplicate-domain errors are shown inline near the form area.

## Visual Checks

- The claims table remains readable on desktop and mobile widths.
- Status and enrollment labels do not overlap action buttons.
- The form is compact and consistent with adjacent admin pages such as Invitations.
